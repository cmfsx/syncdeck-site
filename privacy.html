Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Summary

SyncDeck runs entirely on your computer. It has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We (the developer) do not receive, store, or have access to your files, your Google account details, or any usage data.

What SyncDeck accesses

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google asks you to allow SyncDeck to see and manage files in your Google Drive. SyncDeck uses this permission only to do what you ask: to copy the folders you select between your computer and your own Google Drive, to list and restore those files, and to show your Google Drive as a folder on your computer. SyncDeck also reads your Google account email address to show you which account is connected and to make sure the same account is used for every transfer.

Where your data goes

  • Your files travel only between your computer and your own Google Drive, directly over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • For folders you set to Encrypted private backup, files are encrypted on your computer before upload. Google stores only the encrypted data, and only your recovery key can decrypt it.

What is stored on your computer

  • Your Google sign-in token and your recovery key are stored in your operating system's secure keyring (for example GNOME Keyring or KDE Wallet), not in plain text.
  • A small local database holds your folder list and sync history. Files removed by a sync are kept for 30 days in a local "removed files" folder so you can restore them, then deleted.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SyncDeck's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google is used only to provide and improve the user-facing sync and backup features you request. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others, and not read by humans.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google at any time in the app, or revoke SyncDeck's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove SyncDeck and all its local data with the uninstall option in the app.
  • Files already in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them in Google Drive.

Children

SyncDeck is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published on this page.
