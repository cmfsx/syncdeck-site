Your folders, safely in your own Google Drive

SyncDeck is a free, open-source desktop app that keeps the folders you choose in sync with your own Google Drive, and can keep encrypted private backups of sensitive folders.

What it does

  • Two-way sync - like the Google Drive app, with a safety stop if a sync would delete many files at once.
  • Encrypted private backup - files are scrambled on your computer before they reach Google, and only your 24-word recovery key can open them.
  • Removed files are kept for 30 days and can be restored from inside the app.
  • Live progress for every large file, plus Google Drive shown as a folder in your file manager.

Your data stays yours

SyncDeck has no servers and no accounts. The app talks only to Google, using the permission you grant when you sign in. Nothing is sent to the developer. Read the privacy policy.

Available for Linux (Ubuntu, Debian and compatible). Source code and releases: github.com/cmfsx.
